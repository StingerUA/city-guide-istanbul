# Demo assets

The ten WebP thumbnails in public/images were cropped from screenshots supplied by the project owner. They illustrate a closed prototype and do not establish verified details about any real business. Replace them with licensed originals supplied by participating businesses before public launch. The favicon is an original geometric map-pin mark drawn for this prototype. No third-party font service is loaded.

OpenStreetMap embeds retain provider attribution. Currency data uses ExchangeRate-API with attribution in the converter. External maps and rates require a network connection. No external payment or messaging service is configured.
